AGREED FACTS

The respondent, Ms Dana Whitfield (aged 45), was a juror in a County Court trial in February 2024. The trial judge directed the jury, orally and in writing, not to make any inquiries about the case, including on the internet, and explained why.

On 13 February 2024, during the trial, the respondent searched the internet for the accused's name and found a news report about his prior convictions. In the jury room she told two other jurors what she had found. One of them reported it to the court the next morning. The judge discharged the jury, and the trial had to start again before a new jury three months later, adding to its cost and to the delay for the complainant and the accused.

The respondent was charged with contempt of court. She admitted the contempt at the first hearing and apologised to the court in an affidavit, saying that she had been curious and had not understood how serious it was.

ANTECEDENTS

The respondent has no prior convictions. She works as a school administrator and cares for her elderly mother. She has not been held in custody. No pre-sentence report has been ordered, and there is no psychiatric or psychological report.
