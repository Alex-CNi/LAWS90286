DPP v Hartley (an invented example). On 14 March 2024, outside a hotel in Geelong, the offender, then 31, punched a stranger once in the face and kicked him twice while he lay on the footpath. The victim's jaw and eye socket were fractured and he needed surgery. The offender had been drinking heavily all afternoon and says he remembers little of the assault. He pleaded guilty in the County Court to recklessly causing serious injury at the first opportunity. He has two prior convictions for unlawful assault from 2016 and is not on parole. He has spent 40 days in custody on remand. A pre-sentence report has been ordered.
