AGREED SUMMARY OF FACTS

The accused, Southgate Packaging Pty Ltd, operates a cardboard packaging plant in Campbellfield and employs about 60 people. On 7 February 2022 a production worker, Mr Tomasi Lolo (aged 44), reached into a conveyor to clear a jam. The conveyor's guard had been removed some weeks earlier to make jams easier to clear, and the conveyor had no interlock and no emergency stop within reach. His right arm was drawn into the rollers. He suffered crush injuries and fractures that needed several operations, and he has not returned to work.

WorkSafe's investigation found that the company had not assessed the risks of the conveyor, had not trained its workers in isolation procedures, and knew that the guard was missing. In 2019 an inspector had issued the company with an improvement notice about inadequate guarding on another machine, which it complied with.

The company pleaded guilty at the earliest opportunity to one charge of failing, so far as reasonably practicable, to provide and maintain for its employees a working environment that was safe and without risks to health.

AFTER THE INCIDENT

The company refitted guards and interlocks on all of its conveyors at a cost of about $180,000, engaged an external safety consultant, and introduced lock-out procedures and training. It has topped up Mr Lolo's wages above his WorkCover payments and has apologised to him. Its latest financial statements show annual revenue of about $14 million and a net profit of about $600,000. It has no prior convictions.
