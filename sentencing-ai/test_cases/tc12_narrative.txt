AGREED SUMMARY OF FACTS

On the night of 9 July 2023 the accused, Shane Coulter (then aged 38), broke into a machinery shed on a farm near Kyneton, knowing that the farmer kept his registered firearms there. He used an angle grinder to try to cut open the steel gun safe, which held two rifles and a shotgun. The noise woke the farmer, who called police. The accused fled before he could open the safe, leaving the grinder behind. The safe was badly damaged, but nothing was taken from it.

Police matched DNA on the grinder's handle to the accused. In interview he admitted that he had intended to steal the firearms and sell them. He pleaded guilty at the earliest opportunity to attempted theft of a firearm.

ANTECEDENTS AND OTHER MATERIAL

The accused has prior convictions for burglary and theft (2010, 2014), but none in the eight years before this offence. He is unemployed and has a gambling problem, for which he has started treatment. There is no psychiatric or psychological report. He has been on bail. A pre-sentence report is available, and he consents to a community correction order.
