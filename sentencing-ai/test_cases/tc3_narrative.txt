PROSECUTION SUMMARY (some facts disputed)

In the early hours of 8 February 2025, on the concourse of Flinders Street railway station, the accused, Jordan Ellis (then aged 19), punched a man wearing a high-visibility vest twice in the face after the man asked him to move away from a ticket barrier. The man fell to the ground. He suffered a fractured nose and a cut above his eye that needed stitches.

The prosecution says the man was a Protective Services Officer on duty. The defence says he was a private security contractor and that the accused did not know who he was.

The accused's friends say he had been drinking that night, but there is no evidence of how much he drank.

The accused pleaded guilty to intentionally causing injury on the first day of his trial.

No pre-sentence report has been prepared. The defence has not yet provided any material about the accused's background or circumstances.
