AGREED SUMMARY OF FACTS

At about 3.05 am on 3 June 2023 the accused, Tyler Nguyen (then aged 22), and a co-offender entered a 24-hour service station in Dandenong wearing hooded jumpers and face masks. The accused carried a kitchen knife; the co-offender carried a metal bar. The accused pointed the knife at the console operator, Mr Arjun Patel, and demanded the money from the till and cigarettes. Mr Patel handed over $820 in cash and about $1,500 worth of cigarettes. The co-offender struck the counter with the bar, but no one was injured. The two left in a car driven by a third person.

Police identified the accused from CCTV footage and a fingerprint on the counter. He was arrested four days later and made no comment in interview. He pleaded guilty to armed robbery after the committal hearing, on the agreed basis that he committed the offence in company with the co-offender, who has not been identified.

Mr Patel's victim impact statement describes his fear at work; he has stopped working night shifts.

ANTECEDENTS AND OTHER MATERIAL

The accused has prior convictions for theft and for possessing methylamphetamine, dealt with by a community correction order in 2021, which he completed. He says he committed the robbery to pay a drug debt. He has been in custody since his arrest (about 210 days at the plea hearing) and has taken part in drug counselling in custody. There is no psychiatric or psychological report. A pre-sentence report is available, and he consents to a community correction order.
