SUMMARY OF THE FACTS FOUND AT TRIAL

The accused, Joel Marsh (then aged 27), and the complainant (aged 24) knew each other through friends. On the night of 13 August 2021 both attended a birthday party at a share house in Footscray. Shortly after midnight the complainant, who felt unwell, went to lie down in a spare bedroom and fell asleep fully clothed.

At about 1.30 am on 14 August 2021 the accused entered the room and closed the door. The jury found that he sexually penetrated the complainant while she was asleep, and that he continued after she woke and told him to stop, until she pushed him away. The complainant did not consent, and the accused did not reasonably believe that she consented. She left the house and reported the matter to police later that morning.

The accused had drunk two or three beers during the evening; the prosecution does not allege that he was intoxicated. He denied that any sexual act occurred. He was found guilty of one charge of rape after a five-day trial in the County Court, and he maintains his innocence.

VICTIM IMPACT

The complainant's victim impact statement describes anxiety, disturbed sleep and panic attacks. She withdrew from her university course for a year and moved back to live with her parents.

ANTECEDENTS AND OTHER MATERIAL

The accused has no prior convictions. He works as an electrician and has a supportive family. Character references describe him as reliable and hard-working; the referees know of the verdict. He was on bail until the verdict and has been remanded in custody since then (35 days at the plea in mitigation). A pre-sentence report is available; it notes that he does not accept responsibility for the offending. There is no psychiatric or psychological report.
