AGREED SUMMARY OF FACTS

In March and April 2023 police received reports that a man was selling drugs to students near a secondary school in Werribee. On the afternoon of 26 April 2023 police watched the accused, Mitchell Barker (then aged 29), park his car in the street directly opposite the school's main gate, about 150 metres from the school grounds, shortly after classes finished. Two students in school uniform, aged 15 and 16, approached the car. The accused handed each of them a small bag of methylamphetamine in exchange for $50.

Police stopped the students and then arrested the accused, who made no comment in his police interview. In his car they found 6.2 grams of methylamphetamine divided into 14 small bags, $1,150 in cash, scales, and a mobile phone with messages arranging sales, including messages to the two students. He pleaded guilty to trafficking a drug of dependence after the committal, on the agreed basis that the trafficking took place within 500 metres of a school and included the sales to the two children.

ANTECEDENTS AND OTHER MATERIAL

The accused has prior convictions for possessing and using drugs of dependence (2019, 2021). He has a long history of methylamphetamine use and says that he sold drugs to fund his own use. He has been in custody since his arrest (about 180 days at the plea hearing). There is no psychiatric or psychological report. A pre-sentence report is available, and he consents to a community correction order with drug treatment conditions.
