AGREED SUMMARY OF FACTS

At about 1.20 am on 18 September 2022 the accused, Brodie Walsh (then aged 26), and two co-offenders went to a house in Melton where a family of four were asleep. The accused knew that the occupier, Mr Ivan Petrov, kept cash from his business at the house. The three forced open the rear sliding door and entered. The accused carried a baseball bat, and one co-offender carried a large knife.

Mr Petrov and his wife woke and came into the hallway. The accused threatened Mr Petrov with the bat and demanded the cash, while the co-offender held the knife towards Mrs Petrov. The couple's children, aged 9 and 12, were in their bedrooms. Mr Petrov handed over about $6,000 in cash from a drawer. The offenders left after about ten minutes, taking the cash and two mobile phones. No one was physically injured.

The accused was identified through mobile phone records and arrested two months later. He made no comment in his police interview. He pleaded guilty to aggravated home invasion a week before his trial was due to start. In victim impact statements the Petrov family describe continuing fear in their home; both children have had counselling.

ANTECEDENTS AND OTHER MATERIAL

The accused has prior convictions for burglary and theft (2018) and for recklessly causing injury (2020), for which he served a term of imprisonment; his parole ended in 2021. He has been in custody since his arrest (about 330 days at the plea hearing). No pre-sentence report was ordered, and there is no psychiatric or psychological report.
