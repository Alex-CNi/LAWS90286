AGREED SUMMARY OF FACTS

On the night of 16 November 2024 the accused, Daniel Moretti (then aged 24), was drinking with friends at the Railway Hotel in Footscray. At about 11.40 pm he became involved in an argument with the victim, Mr Samuel Ochieng (aged 31), a stranger, about a spilt drink. Without warning, the accused swung the beer glass he was holding into the left side of the victim's face. The glass broke on impact.

The victim suffered deep lacerations to his left cheek and eyelid. He required surgery under general anaesthetic and 38 sutures. He has permanent facial scarring and reduced sensation in his left cheek.

The accused left the hotel but was identified from CCTV footage and arrested two days later. In a recorded interview he made full admissions. He said he had drunk about ten pots of beer and had "just snapped". He was held in custody for three days before being granted bail.

The accused pleaded guilty at the first available opportunity to one charge of recklessly causing serious injury.

VICTIM IMPACT STATEMENT (tendered without objection)

The victim describes ongoing anxiety in crowded places, embarrassment about his scar, and taking two weeks off work.

PLEA MATERIAL

The accused has no prior convictions. He works full-time as an apprentice electrician. His employer has written a reference describing him as reliable and says his job will be kept open for him. He has written a letter of apology to the victim expressing remorse, and since the offence he has completed six sessions of an alcohol counselling program (confirmed by a letter from the counsellor).

A pre-sentence report assesses him as suitable for a community correction order and at low to moderate risk of reoffending. He consents to a community correction order.
